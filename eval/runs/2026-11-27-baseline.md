Notes
- A first run used a 700-token answer budget. Reasoning models spent it thinking and returned empty text (Granite 22/29, Qwen3.8 8/29), which the harness then counted as answered. Fixed: budget 4096, and an empty answer is now an error. The judge had the same problem with a 10-token cap; it now gets 1,024 tokens and its verdict is parsed by regex.
- All unanswerable questions were abstained by the models themselves (no retrieval gating fired).
- 29 questions is small: one question moves a rate by 3.4 to 4.8 points, so differences under about 10 points are not meaningful.
- MedGemma is not served on the router, so it was not run.
